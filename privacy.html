Privacy Policy - Medved Recorder

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Medved Recorder is a personal tool used only by its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer.

Google data

The app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly solely to upload the owner's screen recordings to the owner's own channel as private videos and to confirm their upload and privacy status. Google authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. No Google user data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
